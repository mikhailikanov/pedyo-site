Safety Guidelines

Staying safe when using Pedyo

For Customers

  • Check a provider's verification badges before hiring
  • Read reviews from previous customers
  • Communicate through Pedyo's messaging system to keep a record
  • Agree on pricing and scope of work before the job begins
  • Meet providers in public or have someone present for first visits
  • Report any suspicious behaviour using the Report button
  • Leave an honest review after your service is complete

For Providers

  • Complete your profile accurately and honestly
  • Submit verification documents to build customer trust
  • Respond to customer messages promptly and professionally
  • Agree on pricing and scope before starting any work
  • Do not share personal financial information through the platform
  • Report any abusive or suspicious customer behaviour
  • Keep your availability and service information up to date

Warning Signs

Be cautious if someone:

  • • Asks for large upfront payments before any work is done
  • • Refuses to communicate through Pedyo's messaging system
  • • Pressures you to make quick decisions
  • • Provides vague or inconsistent information about their services
  • • Asks for personal financial information

How to Report

If you encounter suspicious behaviour, inappropriate content or a safety concern on Pedyo, use the Report button on any provider profile, review or conversation. Our team reviews all reports and takes appropriate action.
